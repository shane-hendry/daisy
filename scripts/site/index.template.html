Daisy’s house style

What sets Daisy apart is its house style for article headers. Every post opens the same way, so a mixed feed is easy to scan and predictable to swipe through. Learn how Daisy’s house style works.

{{FEED}}

Type worth taking in

Daisy and Daisy Sans are typeset with care. Titles scale in size and width to fit their length, quotes hang in the margin, and the eyebrow creates more scannable titles. Generous spacing gives sections room to breathe.

{{IPAD}}

Footnotes for everyone

Authors and book lovers nerd out on footnotes but many readers rarely engage with them or don’t understand how they work, so I designed footnotes that are easy for everyone.

{{FOOTNOTES}}

More niceties

Code blocks get syntax highlighting, language labels, and copy-to-clipboard. Social posts keep each platform’s own conventions, so they look the way you’d expect.

{{NICETIES_1}}

Multi-author bylines follow one predictable format. Smart datelines are relative for the first week, then reveal the exact time with a click. Daisy and Daisy Sans also support over 30 languages, rewriting whole phrases instead of replacing individual words.
